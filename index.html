Privacy Policy

BATTERY JUMP
Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BATTERY JUMP ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you play our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a player profile or use the Game, we may collect the following information:

  • Player name.
  • Country selected by the player.
  • Player profile ID.
  • Device identifier used to create and manage the player profile.
  • Player quote or profile description.
  • Hourly, daily, and weekly game scores.
  • Profile creation and score update timestamps.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage player profiles.
  • Identify existing player profiles.
  • Display player names, countries, quotes, and scores on the leaderboards.
  • Calculate and display leaderboard rankings.
  • Maintain game functionality and server operations.
  • Maintain leaderboard records and historical results.

3. Leaderboard Information

BATTERY JUMP includes online leaderboards. Your player name, selected country, profile quote, and scores may be visible to other players through the Game's leaderboard features.

Certain leaderboard results may be stored in historical leaderboard archives.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Player profile information and game scores are transmitted to and stored on our game backend server to provide online profile and leaderboard functionality.

We do not sell players' personal information. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics services in the current version of the Game.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers involved in operating the Game's server.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of BATTERY JUMP does not contain advertisements and does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. Data Retention and Profile Deletion

We retain player information for as long as necessary to provide the Game's features and maintain its records.

Players can use the in-game profile deletion feature to request deletion of their active player profile. Some historical leaderboard records may be retained separately.

For questions or additional data deletion requests, please contact us using the contact information below.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information transmitted to and stored on our backend server. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the information necessary for the Game's stated features. If you believe that a child has provided information inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
